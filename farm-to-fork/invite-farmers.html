Have you ever wondered how to get your products onto the menus of local restaurants, schools, hospitals, caterers, or other food businesses? The Farm to Fork Exchange is built specifically to connect farmers and food producers directly with the chefs and buyers who are looking for more locally sourced products.

Join us for an evening of great food, meaningful conversations, and new business opportunities — enjoying a menu prepared by local chefs using local ingredients while meeting the buyers who want to purchase more fresh, local products throughout the year.

During the evening, you'll

🌱

Meet chefs, restaurants, caterers, schools, and institutional buyers

🥕

Learn exactly what products local buyers are looking for

📈

Discover how to prepare your business for wholesale opportunities

💬

Share your products, growing practices, and seasonal availability

🛒

Learn about new systems making it easier to connect with buyers year-round

Attend, and take home $75 in Farmers Market tokens.

Every farmer or food producer who attends the September 22 Farm to Fork Exchange farm-to-table meal receives $75 in Muskegon Farmers Market tokens — our thank-you for taking an evening to invest in these relationships.

Tokens are provided on-site at check-in the evening of the event.
